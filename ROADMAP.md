# ClearboxGTM roadmap

This roadmap separates product proof, repository distribution, and campaign activation so a release never silently becomes an outreach send.

## Phase 1 — Client-pack showcase (`v0.10.0`)

- Redesign the generated eleven-view Sheet dashboard.
- Publish a synthetic, view-only demonstration Sheet and guided Notion brief.
- Show the full API → analysis → working Sheet → readable brief path in the README.
- Ship a reproducible GIF/MP4 walkthrough made from verified product captures.
- Keep Clearbox disposition, exact Reddit permalink, human authorization, and evidence-level boundaries intact.

## Phase 2 — Broader skill distribution

- Refresh the linked Reddit chapter, starter, and skills in [`gtm-coding-agent`](https://github.com/shawnla90/gtm-coding-agent).
- Keep the portable GTM patterns available in the broader curriculum.
- Route builders who want the complete Reddit-growth implementation into this repository.
- Publish a separate `gtm-coding-agent` release only after its mirrored contracts and links are current.

## Phase 3 — Repository launch campaign

This phase is plan-first and approval-gated. Before any audience is activated:

1. Reconcile existing email, social, community, product-user, reply, trial, out-of-office, and suppression states from their actual systems of record.
2. Read the most recent conversation context before deciding who should receive a message and which channel owns it.
3. Dedupe people across channels without guessing ambiguous identities.
4. Write channel-native messages that lead with the repository and skills, not a generic product pitch.
5. Decide the sender identity and domain from current deliverability and relationship evidence.
6. Build a private Notion campaign plan containing reach, source receipts, messaging, approval gates, measurement, and suppression logic.
7. Obtain explicit approval for the audience, copy, sender, timing, and send mechanism.

Repository releases are allowed to notify repository watchers through GitHub's native release channel. Direct messages, group posts, email campaigns, and social sends remain separate human-authorized actions.

## Measurement contract

- **Repository delivery:** release published, links render, assets load.
- **Reach:** recipient or platform delivery state, not an assumed impression.
- **Response:** positive, neutral, not interested, out of office, or no reply.
- **Progression:** active conversation, trial, opportunity, conversion, or suppression.
- **Content visibility:** search discovery, observed AI appearance, and exact citation remain distinct.
- **Business outcome:** connect any opportunity or revenue event back to its original source receipt.
