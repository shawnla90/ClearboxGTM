# Students

Build a GTM track record before anyone gives you a title. If you're a student who builds in public, Clearbox Pro is free for a semester — plus a monthly group office-hours session with Shawn to unblock whatever you're stuck on.

## The offer

- **A free semester of Clearbox Pro.** The grant is measured in *time, never volume* — you get the full product for the semester. The five-tracked-subreddit cap stays, on purpose: it's anti-abuse design, and it's also the right constraint for learning. Five communities, deeply read, beat fifty skimmed.
- **Monthly group office hours.** One live group session a month with Shawn: bring your account, your queue, your stuck thread.

## How to activate

1. Sign up at [clearbox.to](https://clearbox.to) with your **.edu email**.
2. Email **partners@clearbox.to** from that same .edu address with one line about what you're building. That flips the student grant on.

## The semester arc

The offer isn't the point — the track record is. The arc that produces one:

1. **Week 1: build your offer pack.** Pick a campus org or a local business and run the offer-context interview for them ([`playbooks/offer-context-onboarding.md`](playbooks/offer-context-onboarding.md)). The source-or-cut discipline is the first lesson, and the five tracked communities you pick map exactly onto the grant's five-subreddit cap.
2. **Run the engine on one community.** Read it daily through Clearbox, engage as a human where you're actually useful ([`skills/reddit-engage/`](skills/reddit-engage/)), let everything off-platform be automated ([`playbooks/how-to-win-on-reddit.md`](playbooks/how-to-win-on-reddit.md)).
3. **Keep a win ledger.** Every real outcome — a reply that helped someone, a thread that ranked, a lead, a thank-you — with a permalink. Generated-not-typed applies to you too: if you can't link it, it didn't happen.
4. **Publish the receipts.** Ship the semester as a public write-up with the ledger attached. That artifact is worth more than the line on your resume it replaces.
