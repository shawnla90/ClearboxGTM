# Win an agency client — the reverse-uno method

Agencies get pitched all day. The move this playbook teaches is the reversal: when a prospect (or their agency) talks channels at you, you show them what their *buyers* are already saying — on Reddit, this week, in their own words — and let the evidence do the selling. You stop pitching a service and start handing over a readout they can check line by line.

## 1. The reverse-uno

When you're pitched — or when you're the agency pitching — the answer is the same sentence: **"You should be reading your clients' buyers on Reddit."** Then you prove it's not a slogan:

1. Run the offer-context interview *for the prospect's client* ([`offer-context-onboarding.md`](offer-context-onboarding.md)). The first deliverable is an offer pack built from their market — researched competitors, buyer-language keywords, verified communities — before any contract exists. It proves you read their market before you pitched.
2. Pull a Clearbox readout against that pack: live buyer threads, classified by intent, with permalinks. Real people, real timestamps, their exact words.
3. Hand both over. An audit they can falsify beats a deck they have to believe.

The full packaged version of this motion — buyer-signal sheet, deck, Notion command center — is [`../skills/reddit-agency/`](../skills/reddit-agency/).

## 2. First-month scope

What the first month actually contains (scope, not pricing — terms are between you and your client):

- **Identity setup.** Real account, real operator, disclosure decided up front. The account/workspace/operator boundary from the [multi-account operations guide](../skills/reddit-agency/MULTI-ACCOUNT-OPERATIONS.md) is the contract's technical annex.
- **Community research.** The offer pack's tracked communities, verified and prioritized — buyer rooms first.
- **A useful-answer queue.** Drafted replies to live threads, every one value-first, every one waiting on a human approve. This is explicitly **not karma farming**: the account earns its standing by being useful in public, or the whole motion is fake.
- **Human approvals.** Nothing posts automatically. The client sees the queue; a person presses send.
- **Weekly evidence.** What was engaged, what it earned, with permalinks — evidence the client can check, not activity reports they have to trust.
- **30/60/90 measurement.** Presence first, then engagement quality, then attributed outcomes. The five-level scorecard in the multi-account guide is the maturity ladder.

## 3. Win evidence

Bring receipts that aren't yours to argue with: the numbers in [`../proof/`](../proof/) are generated from a tracking database by [`../proof/generate_proof.py`](../proof/generate_proof.py), never typed, and [`../skills/reddit-agency/MULTI-ACCOUNT-EVIDENCE.md`](../skills/reddit-agency/MULTI-ACCOUNT-EVIDENCE.md) documents the operating evidence behind the multi-account guide. When you make this pitch yourself, hold your own numbers to the same provenance rule — a generated stat survives due diligence; a typed one is a liability with a delay on it.
