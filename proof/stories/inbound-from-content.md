# Inbound from content

The pattern: genuine replies attract genuine responses. Those responses compound into relationships, and a fraction of those relationships become pipeline.

This is not a growth hack. It is the slowest possible way to build pipeline, and it works because it is real.

## The numbers

All from `proof-data.json`, generated by `proof/generate_proof.py`:

- **724 inbound replies** across 246 items
- **25 tracked wins** (items where the conversation led to a measurable outcome)
- **5.4 months** tracked

That is roughly 3 inbound replies per item that received any response at all, and a win rate of about 1 in 10 on responded items.

## How it compounds

The two-era breakdown from the proof pipeline shows the compounding:

**Karma-building era** (37.9 days):
- 422 items posted
- 1.19M views
- High volume, broad presence, building karma and history

**Working era** (121.3 days):
- 368 items posted
- 378K views
- Lower volume, but every post carries the weight of 2,503 karma and months of genuine participation

The shift matters. In the karma-building era, you are earning the right to be taken seriously. In the working era, you already have it. The same comment from a 2,500-karma account with months of history lands differently than the same comment from a new account. Reddit users check post history. AI models read the full thread. Both reward consistency.

## What "inbound" means here

An inbound reply is someone responding to your comment or post with engagement — a question, a follow-up, a "thanks, that helped," a "can you tell me more." It is not a like or an upvote. It is someone choosing to continue the conversation with you.

724 of those across 246 items means the account consistently generated conversations, not just impressions. The conversations are where relationships start, and relationships are where pipeline comes from.

## The pipeline

The deal pipeline from the proof data:

- 46 open
- 10 trial
- 7 won
- 62 lost

129 signups tracked by first-touch channel: 57 referral, 51 web, 8 LinkedIn, 7 search, 5 email, 1 X.

The referral channel is the largest first-touch source. Referrals do not come from ads or cold outbound. They come from someone who saw value, remembered it, and passed it along. That is what genuine Reddit presence produces at scale — not direct conversions, but a network of people who know what you do because you helped them once.

## What this does not prove

This does not prove that Reddit is the best channel for every business. It proves that genuine participation in buyer conversations compounds into pipeline for this specific account, in this specific market, over 5.4 months.

The transparency folder ([what-actually-worked.md](../../transparency/what-actually-worked.md)) documents the honest channel ranking, including the channels that did not work. The cold-email postmortem is there too. Selectivity in what you claim is part of the method.
